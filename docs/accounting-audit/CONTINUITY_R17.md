- **Lost (never committed):** the R17 section of `TEST_DATA.md`, `round17-main.cjs`, `round17-snapshot.cjs`, `evidence/round-17/*` (22 stages of screenshots / responses / DB snapshots), `.scratch/r17/main.log`, and the database itself (the R10–R16 ledger). - Root cause of the loss: evidence for 21 stages was kept on disk for too long before `git commit` + `git push`. Standing rule from R17 onwards: **commit and push evidence after every slice (≤ 5 write stages); nothing audit-relevant may exist only in the working tree or `/tmp`.**
